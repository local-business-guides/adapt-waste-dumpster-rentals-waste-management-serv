What the service covers and how to plan it

Start with a clear description of your project. Note the material, approximate amount, where it is located, and whether a truck or container can reach the spot. Then ask the provider to confirm the service, timing and any restrictions before work begins.

Key facts to clarify:
- Coverage: Dumpster rental, debris removal, junk removal, garbage collection, equipment rental and waste management are listed services. Ask which one applies to your job.
- Timing: The schedule depends on availability, the service selected, the rental period if you need a dumpster, and the requested pickup or removal date.
- Cost factors: In general, the container size, rental length, material type, access and pickup needs can affect the cost. Request details for your own job rather than relying on a general estimate.
- Accepted material: Check what can go in the container or be collected. Do not assume every item is accepted.

A few terms help make calls easier. A dumpster rental means using a container for a set period. Debris removal refers to taking away material from a project or cleanup. Haul-away means the provider removes items rather than leaving a container for you to fill.
