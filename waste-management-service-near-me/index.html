Key facts before booking

A useful request includes the location, the type and amount of material, where it sits, and your preferred timing. Share access details too, such as a gate, stairs, limited parking or a tight placement area. Ask the provider to confirm the service and arrangements before you rely on a date.

What can affect the plan:
- Service type: Dumpster rental, junk removal and debris removal are different ways to handle a cleanup. Confirm which one is being discussed.
- Timing: The provider’s availability, the work involved, rental duration and pickup date all affect scheduling. Ask for the timing that applies to your job.
- Cost in general: Container size, how long you need it, material type, access and pickup needs can affect the total. Request job-specific details; there is no single cost that fits every cleanup.
- Material rules: Ask what is accepted before loading a container or arranging collection.

Here are a few terms you may hear. Rental period is the time a container is assigned for use. Pickup is the planned collection of that container or collected material. Access describes whether the provider can reach the drop-off or removal location.
