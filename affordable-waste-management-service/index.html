Service details, cost factors, and terms

The right approach depends on the kind of cleanup and the service you want. For example, ask whether a dumpster rental or a removal service is more appropriate for your situation. Check exactly what the provider will handle, since the names of the services don't spell out accepted items, restrictions, or included tasks.

Key facts to discuss:
- Service scope: Confirm whether you are asking about a container, pickup, debris removal, junk removal, or another listed service, and what that includes.
- Timing: Ask about the schedule for your project. Timing can vary with provider availability, the requested service, site access, and the length of time the job requires.
- Cost factors: The service selected, type and quantity of material, access, duration, and any additional handling may affect cost. Get a project-specific explanation rather than assuming one service has a fixed price.
- Provider comparison: Request the same information from each provider: what is included, when the work can happen, what materials are accepted, and what could add to the cost. A low initial figure may not describe the same scope as another provider's offer.

Common terms are straightforward: container means the receptacle used to hold waste; debris removal means taking leftover material away; junk removal concerns unwanted items; and garbage collection refers to pickup of garbage. Ask a provider to explain any additional terms in its own service description.
